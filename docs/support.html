Where is my data?

Everything stays on your iPhone and is included in your regular iPhone backup. Export a PDF from any home to keep a copy somewhere else.

I bought Pro on another device

Open Settings in Roomprint and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

The label scanner didn't read my sticker

Get closer, turn on more light, and keep the phone steady. You can always type the model or serial number by hand.

Want us to record your home?

In central Georgia, Roomprint can visit and record your home for you in about an hour.
