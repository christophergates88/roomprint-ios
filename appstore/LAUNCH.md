## Facebook / Nextdoor posts (pick one a week)

**1. The question**
If your house burned tonight, could you list everything in it? After a fire, storm or break-in, the insurer
asks for a list of what you lost, with values. Most people forget half of it. I built Roomprint so you can film
each room once and get that list, with brands and replacement values, on your iPhone. First room scan is free.
getroomprint.com

**2. The garage**
Quick one: walk your garage with your phone for 60 seconds. Mower, tools, bikes, freezer, kayaks. Most people
are surprised what it adds up to. Roomprint lists it for you and keeps it on your phone, not in someone's cloud.
Free to try: getroomprint.com

**3. Before storm season**
Storm season tip: do your home inventory before you need it. Film each room, approve the list, save the PDF
somewhere outside the house (email it to yourself). Twenty minutes now saves weeks of guessing later.
getroomprint.com

Photo ideas you can take yourself: your own garage shelf, a room from the doorway, the app's total on your phone.
